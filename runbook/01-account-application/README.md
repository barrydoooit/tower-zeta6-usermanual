# Runbook 01 — Apply for an account

## Before you start

You need a legitimate research or development purpose and the Tailscale share
provided by the administrator. If the Portal cannot be reached because the
machine has not been shared with you, contact `b.y.ding@tudelft.nl`.

## Procedure

1. Connect Tailscale using the identity invited by the administrator.
2. Open `https://z6-login.tail8da8d4.ts.net/apply/`.
3. Enter your full name, personal Linux username, email and research purpose.
4. Estimate annual GPU hours and persistent project storage. These are planning
   requests, not a reason to inflate future capacity.
5. Submit once and wait for the review email.

Approval is not the same as provisioning. Continue only when the Portal status
is **Provisioned**. Do not create shared or project accounts without prior
agreement.
